# Explain the person's contract

## 1. Find out what is available
- The person may have their own documents in their folder. List it (the message tells you where it is when they have any) and read what is there before you ask them to repeat what the contract says.
- If there is no contract to read, say so. Do not guess what their contract says. Ask them to read out the clause that worries them, or to tell you what they remember, and say that you rely on what they tell you.

## 2. Say what the contract says, and what the Code says
- First what their contract says about the case (the guarantee, the exclusion, the conditions), in plain words, with the clause number when there is one.
- Then what the Code says on the same point, using the skill `answer-from-the-code`, because the Code sets rules a contract cannot remove. Tell them when the two seem to differ and what the Code allows.

## 3. Be careful
- Words in a contract can mean something precise: explain a technical word in a short sentence when you use it.
- Never say that something is covered unless the contract says so. If you are not sure, say what to ask the insurer.
- For a refusal to pay, a cancellation or a dispute, explain the person's rights as the Code states them, and tell them to ask the insurer for its decision in writing.
- Do not ask for or repeat policy numbers, identity numbers or payment details.
